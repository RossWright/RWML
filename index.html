Ross Wright Metal Libraries

.NET foundations from Pross Co.

A suite of packages for dependency injection, mediator-style commands, utilities, auth, and HTTP endpoints — usable on their own or together, on server, Blazor WebAssembly, and console.

Packages target .NET 8, 9, and 10 (with a few provider exceptions called out in the docs).
